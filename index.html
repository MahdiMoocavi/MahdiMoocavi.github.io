FEATURED NLP PROJECT / 01

How can geometric methods improve transformer-based sentence embeddings?

NLP · TRANSFORMER EMBEDDINGS · REPRESENTATION ANALYSIS

Sentence embedding analysis and geometric correction

This NLP pipeline uses Sentence Transformers and the pretrained all-mpnet-base-v2 model to encode 300 IPIP-NEO-300 statements as 768-dimensional vectors. It builds pairwise cosine-similarity matrices and examines how shared linguistic features affect semantic relationships between items, particularly reverse-keyed pairs within the same facet.

The Python implementation compares eight conditions: raw embeddings and seven corrections, including global mean-centering, PCA-based component removal (drop-top / all-but-the-top), regularised ZCA and partial whitening, domain- and facet-informed centering, and nested pair-scope corrections. This separates geometry-only methods from corrections that use known domain and facet metadata.

Evaluation covers 696 predefined within-facet reverse-keyed pairs and 654 same-keyed controls. The best reported condition, nested full-rescale, reaches 90.5% sign-accuracy against a theoretical negative-sign expectation. This is an embedding-space result—not evidence that corrected similarities reproduce empirical item-response correlations. Response-based validation remains outstanding.

02

EXPERIMENT DESIGN / DECISION MODELLING

Sequential Choice Dynamics: Lagged-Predictor Generalized Linear Mixed Models

A PsychoPy experiment records trial-level choices, accuracy, response times, and confidence. The companion R workflow combines 72 participant-level files, constructs within-participant lagged predictors, and fits linear and logistic mixed-effects models. It also generates model-based confidence estimates for trials without an observed rating.

DATA PIPELINE / META-ANALYSIS

Multi-Study Data Harmonisation and Effect-Size Synthesis

A Python pipeline ingests study-specific Excel exports, applies participant filters, harmonises variables, constructs derived measures, and records reviewable text-normalisation suggestions in an audit trail. A separate R workflow combines results from 65 English- and Dutch-language groups using paired effect sizes, equal- and random-effects models, and Fisher's z-transformed correlations.

Latent-Variable Modelling: CFA, SEM and Mediation Analysis

An R workflow that prepares survey measures, checks distributions and multicollinearity, fits confirmatory factor and structural equation models, estimates indirect effects, and uses bootstrap parameter estimates. A hold-out split compares model fit; it is not a predictive-accuracy benchmark.

PROFILE / NLP / STATISTICS / DATA SCIENCE

Statistical modelling grounded in psychology and computation.

I hold an MSc in Psychology: Theory & Research from KU Leuven, where my training focused on quantitative research methods and advanced statistics. My research background combines experimental design, programming, statistical inference, and computational modelling. I am particularly interested in computational psychometrics, semantic representations of psychological constructs, and methods that connect language-based models with response-based measurement.

My work moves between substantive questions and their quantitative representation: defining measurable constructs, preparing data, specifying statistical models, evaluating assumptions and sensitivity, and interpreting uncertainty. My experience includes Python- and R-based workflows, regression, Bayesian and multilevel modelling, meta-analysis, latent-variable analysis, experimental measurement, and NLP-based representation analysis.
